# Xiaomi AX3000T Stock Firmware Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans task by task.

**Goal:** Deliver stock-layout MT7981 AX3000T firmware including MediaTek EasyMesh support for AX6000 interoperability.
**Architecture:** Isolated warm native build with MT7981 config and hardware-aware Mesh runtime. Validate produced firmware, not only its recipe.
**Tech Stack:** OpenWrt Make/Kconfig, vendor mt_wifi, APK, shell/ucode LuCI.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-31-ax3000t-stock-design.md

## Global Constraints
- MediaTek RD03/RD23 only; stock bootloader and partition layout.
- Recommended 25.12 / 6.12.108 / mt_wifi 7.6.7.3, MT7981 firmware.
- No ASU or embedded Tailscale; no unrelated host/router changes.
- No transfer of AX6000-only kernel/vendor packages or packet-test attestations.

## Review Focus
- MT7981 and MT7986 package-name collisions must not preserve stale binary payloads.
- Both MT7531 and AN8855/Foresee hardware support must remain in kernel/DTS.
- Factory kernel and rootfs must fit separate 34MiB and 78MiB UBI partitions.
- Mesh snapshots, MU reload and rollback must address the correct DAT files.
- Real hardware interoperability remains unproven until tested on AX3000T.
